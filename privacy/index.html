Privacy Policy

Sledpaws: Snow Slide · Effective date: 16 October 2026

The short version

  • Sledpaws needs no account. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • This version of the game has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it sends nothing about you to us or to anyone else.
  • Your progress (coins, gems, upgrades, maps, settings) is saved only on your device.
  • If you email us, we use your message only to answer you.

1. Who we are

Sledpaws: Snow Slide (“Sledpaws”, “the game”) is made by BEYDANUR DONMEZ (Beydanur Dönmez), an individual developer (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the game on the Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad) and on Google Play (Android phones and tablets), and this website. You can reach us at sledpaws.sandbox@outlook.com.

2. What the game stores

The game saves your progress on your device so you can carry on where you left off: coin and gem balances, upgrade levels, unlocked maps and characters, best distances and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language). This data never leaves your device through the game. It contains nothing that identifies you.

Your phone or tablet may include the game's saved progress in its own device backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad, Android backup on Android), depending on your device settings. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies, not by us; we cannot see them.

3. What we do not collect

The game does not collect or send personal data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos or usage analytics. It does not use cookies or tracking. It does not ask for any device permission. Vibration (haptics) works on the device itself and sends nothing.

4. App Store and Google Play

When you download the game, Apple or Google process your account and download information under their own privacy policies. They may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example the number of downloads per country and crash reports from people who chose to share them with developers). These statistics do not tell us who you are.

5. When you email us

If you write to sledpaws.sandbox@outlook.com, we receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. We use it only to answer you, and we delete the conversation within 12 months after it ends unless the law requires us to keep it. Our email provider (Microsoft Outlook) processes the messages for us.

6. Children

Sledpaws is a cartoon game suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal data, it collects none from children either. If you think a child has sent us personal information by email, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, the KVKK in Turkey or US state laws), you have rights to access, correct and delete your personal data and to object to its use. Since the game keeps your progress only on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the game. For anything we may hold because you emailed us, write to sledpaws.sandbox@outlook.com and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Future versions

A later version may add optional ads (for example a rewarded ad you choose to watch for bonus coins) or in-app purchases. Before any such version is released, we will update this policy to describe exactly what changes, change the effective date above and mention it in the update notes. Nothing described in this section is in the game today.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top always shows the latest version.
