Support

Need help with Sledpaws: Snow Slide? Most answers are below. If yours isn't, write to sledpaws.sandbox@outlook.com and tell us your device model and the game version (shown in Settings).

How to play

How do I launch the sled?

Touch the screen, drag down to pull the slingshot rope back, and let go. The further you pull, the faster you start.

How do I steer?

While sliding, drag your finger left or right anywhere on the screen.

What do flips do?

Ramps send you into the air. While airborne, swipe up to flip. Land cleanly and you get a short speed boost.

When does a run end?

When your sled stops. Bumping into obstacles slows you down. Reach the finish line to unlock the next map.

Upgrades and progress

Do my upgrades reset on a new map?

No. Slingshot, Sled and Income upgrades are permanent and stay with you on every map.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud save. If you delete the game, your progress (including coins and gems you bought) is deleted too. A device backup (iCloud Backup or Android backup) may bring it back when you restore that backup to a device.

Purchases and ads

How do I restore my purchases?

Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account (iPhone, iPad) or Google Account (Android) you bought with, then tap Restore Purchases in the game's Settings. This brings back Remove Ads. Gems and items you already received are part of your progress on the device and can't be restored after the game was deleted.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play, so refunds are handled by them: ask Apple for a refund or ask Google Play for a refund.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Rewarded ads (for example “x3 coins” or free gems) are always your choice. Full-screen ads appear at most after every 4th run, never before your 5th run and never right after you complete a map. Remove Ads turns them off.

Privacy

How do I change my ad and privacy choices?

Open Settings → Privacy in the game. On iPhone and iPad you can also change tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. The game works the same whatever you choose. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Why does the game ask my age?

To choose the right settings. Players under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in their country), and players who don't answer, get only ads suitable for children, no tracking prompt and limited analytics. The answer stays on your device.

How do I ask for my data to be deleted?

Most data is only on your device: deleting the game deletes it. Analytics and crash data held by Google are linked only to a random app ID; a new installation gets a new ID, and you can also reset your advertising ID. For any other request, or to ask us to delete data, write to sledpaws.sandbox@outlook.com. See the Privacy Policy.

Settings

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and use the switches.

How do I change the language?

The game follows your device language. You can also pick a language in Settings.

Contact

Email: sledpaws.sandbox@outlook.com. We usually answer within a few days.
