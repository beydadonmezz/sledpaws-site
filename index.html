Sledpaws

Pull back the slingshot, let go and slide! A cat in a cosy scarf rides a sled down icy tracks, collects coins, flips off ramps and races for the finish line.

Coming soon as “Sledpaws: Snow Slide” to the App Store (iPhone and iPad) and Google Play (Android)
  • Pull, release, slideStretch the rope, let go and zoom down the ice. Drag left and right to steer.
  • Flip for speedRamps throw you into the air. Land a clean flip and you get a little burst of speed.
  • Upgrades that staySpend coins on a stronger slingshot, a faster sled and better income. Your upgrades are yours to keep on every map.
  • New places to slideSnowy villages, frozen lakes, cities and caves, each with something new on the track.
  • Cute ridersStart with the scarf-wearing cat and meet more animal friends later.
  • No sign-upNo account, no email, no password. Your progress is saved on your device.
